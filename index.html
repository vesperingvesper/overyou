One tap for the shoebox-magnifier setup: rotates the image 180° to pre-cancel the lens flip and switches Ceiling mode on. A single lens rotates the picture but doesn't mirror it, so Mirror stays off — fine-tune with Rotate if your box sits at an angle.
For projecting onto a ceiling: turn on Ceiling mode, then use Rotate / Mirror / Flip to line the planets up with how your mirror or lens throws the image. Brightness drives the ceiling-mode contrast.
